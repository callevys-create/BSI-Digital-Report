Import ring allocations

Upload .xlsx or UTF-8 CSV, one breed per row. Several rows may use the same ring. Required columns: Ring, Judge, Breed. Maximum 2 MB and 2,000 rows. Only use plain cell values. Imports replace the active list for the selected show and preserve earlier revisions.

Review allocation rows

Review every unrecognised breed. Choose the correct BSI breed, or explicitly confirm “Not BSI-listed”. No unrecognised breed is silently classified as not required.
